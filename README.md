# 🔮 PROPHET AI

### Multi-Agent Market Intelligence Platform for Indian Stock Markets

PROPHET AI is an AI-powered market intelligence platform designed to analyze Indian financial markets using multiple specialized research agents working together.

The platform combines technical analysis, quantitative research, options intelligence, market sentiment, fundamentals, macro factors, risk analysis, and multi-agent AI reasoning into a unified trading research environment.

## 🧠 Multi-Agent Architecture

PROPHET AI divides market research into specialized AI agents:

Technical → Options → Quant → Fundamental → Macro → Intelligence → Risk

Each agent produces structured analysis and evidence.

The results are then evaluated through a multi-agent debate and validation process before the final **Master CIO** engine generates a consolidated market view.

## 📡 Signal Intelligence

Every signal is designed around:

- Market Direction
- Entry Zone
- Stop Loss
- Target Levels
- Risk/Reward
- Confidence Score
- Supporting Evidence
- Invalidation Conditions
- Signal Lifecycle
- Outcome Tracking

The system is designed to provide **probabilistic research and decision support**, not guaranteed predictions.
